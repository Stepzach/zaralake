A little bit about me

Good songs, good people & a good singalong.

I’m Zara, a 22-year-old acoustic singer and guitarist performing a mix of acoustic pop, folk-pop and classic favourites.

From Brown Eyed Girl by Van Morrison to I’m Yours by Jason Mraz, I love playing songs people know, love and can sing along to.

I’m currently looking to book more gigs and would love to play at your pub, party, venue, festival or event.
